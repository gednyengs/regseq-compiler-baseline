### What `regseq-backends-16` shipped (`dad9a1e` redesign + `3f2a172` soundness fix)

§8 diag-log lowering re-shaped from a single NUL-terminated `printf(fmt,args)` to a **uniform
`fwrite` statement sequence** (every byte to stdout via `fwrite(ptr,1,n,stdout)` with an explicit
count). `emit_diag_log` (`body_emit.rs` ~1961): a `pending` literal accumulator flushed before
each placeholder (tag merges into the leading run, `\n` into the trailing run); **numeric** →
`{ char <nbuf>[24]; int <nlen> = snprintf(<nbuf>, sizeof <nbuf>, "<spec>", <cast>(<expr>));
fwrite(<nbuf>,1,<nlen>,stdout); }` (`[24]` provably > the 20-byte max rendering → no over-read;
`<nbuf>`/`<nlen>` from `fresh_temp_name`); **static `&str`** → `fwrite("<CMS bytes>",1,<decoded
len>,stdout)`; **non-static `&str`** → `fwrite((<expr>).data,1,(<expr>).len,stdout)` (no
buffer/cap/truncation). The §8.3 256-cap truncation contract is **removed** (a `&str` of any
length prints in full). `c_escape`'s `double_percent` param dropped. The specifier+cast mapping
(`diag_specifier_and_cast_for`), template parser, and static-vs-non-static discriminator are
**reused unchanged**. Fixtures: re-blessed all goldens; `diag_str_truncation`→`diag_str_long`
(≥256 prints in full); **+`diag_nul`** (interior NUL on all three paths; `expected.stdout` carries
literal NUL bytes, byte-exact via the NUL-valid-UTF-8 oracle).
