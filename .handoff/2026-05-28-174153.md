### What `regseq-backends-16` shipped (`dad9a1e` redesign + `3f2a172` soundness fix)

The §8 diag-log lowering re-shaped from a single NUL-terminated `printf(fmt,args)` to a
**uniform `fwrite` statement sequence** (every byte to stdout via `fwrite(ptr,1,n,stdout)` with
an explicit count). `emit_diag_log` (`body_emit.rs` ~1961): a `pending` literal accumulator
flushed before each placeholder (tag merges into the leading run, `\n` into the trailing run);
**numeric** placeholder → `{ char <nbuf>[24]; int <nlen> = snprintf(<nbuf>, sizeof <nbuf>,
"<spec>", <cast>(<expr>)); fwrite(<nbuf>,1,<nlen>,stdout); }` (the `[24]` buffer is provably >
the 20-byte max admissible rendering → no over-read; `<nbuf>`/`<nlen>` from `fresh_temp_name`);
**static `&str`** → `fwrite("<CMS bytes>",1,<decoded len>,stdout)`; **non-static `&str`** →
`fwrite((<expr>).data,1,(<expr>).len,stdout)` (no buffer/cap/truncation). The §8.3 256-cap
truncation contract is **removed** (a `&str` of any length prints in full). `c_escape`'s
`double_percent` param dropped (literals are `fwrite`n raw). The specifier+cast mapping
(`diag_specifier_and_cast_for`), template parser, and static-vs-non-static discriminator are
**reused unchanged**. Fixtures: re-blessed all goldens; `diag_str_truncation`→`diag_str_long`
(≥256 prints in full); **+`diag_nul`** (interior NUL on all three paths; `expected.stdout` carries
literal NUL bytes, byte-exact via the NUL-valid-UTF-8 oracle).

## Key decisions & rationale

(Durable list: `decisions-and-state.md` — the `-16` close + the soundness fix are recorded.)

- **Uniform `fwrite` (architect's fork choice) over minimal/segmented.** Single write mechanism;
  numerics via `snprintf`→`fwrite`. The "minimal" option (keep `printf`, fwrite only `&str`
  values) was rejected — it leaves the template-literal interior NUL unhandled.
- **The §8 redesign falsifies generic-C-binding *prose* in both docs** → narrow corrections to
  `generic-c-backend.md` §1/§7.1/§8 + `spec.md` §26.1/§26.8/§26.9/§26.10 (a soundness fix can't
  leave contradictory normative text). The IR grammar (§26.7), the peel contract (§26.6), the
  D-rule values, and the Pipeline-A prohibition are **unchanged** (the IR always allowed
  interior-NUL `&str`; the fix is the C-target binding).

  names, incl. `debug_name`-derived). Hardcoded temp names are unsound — they silently shadow.
  This was the 6th soundness-pass catch; the discipline is encoded in `fresh_temp_name`'s doc +
  now in `emit_diag_log`.
