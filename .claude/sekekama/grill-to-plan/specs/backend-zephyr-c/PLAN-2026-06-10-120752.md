# PLAN — backend-zephyr-c (Zephyr PL011 first-light unit)

## Overview

Implement the `zephyr_c` backend (independent lowering of `RegseqModel` to an idiomatic Zephyr driver TU + generated DT binding yaml, per the F-1 contract) and prove it end-to-end on the `pl011-composed-zephyr` sibling fixture through a Zephyr-flavored L0–L3 ladder, plus the in-gate authored-`Err` live witness. The machine-checked identity invariant (C02) ties the two OS fixtures to one spec. Unit merges at Zephyr PL011 first light (GRILL C01); merge is the planning trigger for both successors (D03/D16). Source of truth: PRD-2026-06-10-114302 (R01–R14, I01–I10, A1–A3) and GRILL-2026-06-10-113921 (D01–D21, C01–C05, S01–S16, O1/O2).
